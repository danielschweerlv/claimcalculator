# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-04-30*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Free AI-powered car accident settlement estimator and lead-generation funnel for Nevada personal injury cases.

**What it does:** ClaimCalculator.ai helps injured drivers estimate the possible value of an auto-accident claim, compare likely outcomes with and without an attorney, and submit their information for follow-up. The product is both a consumer-facing calculator and the front door for qualified personal-injury leads.

**Product category:** Personal injury settlement calculator; car accident claim estimator; legal lead-generation funnel.

**Product type:** Web app / lead-generation product.

**Business model:** Captures qualified leads for personal injury attorneys.

## Target Audience

**Target users:** Nevada and Las Vegas drivers who were injured in a car accident and are trying to understand the possible value of their claim.

**Downstream buyer:** Personal injury attorneys who want qualified local auto-accident leads.

**Primary use case:** Turn uncertainty after an accident into a quick, credible settlement estimate and attorney-intake path.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Estimate a possible settlement range from accident and injury inputs.
- Understand why attorney representation may change claim outcomes.
- Capture lead information for attorney follow-up.

## Messaging Notes

- Lead with clarity and trust, not hype.
- Keep legal disclaimers visible and plain.
- Avoid implying a guaranteed settlement outcome.
- The calculator should feel precise, fast, and legally sober.

## Design Voice

The project-local design system defines the direction as "Neon Nocturne / Digital Concierge": dark, cinematic, data-confident, technical, and authoritative without becoming sterile. Read `DESIGN.md` before writing UI or marketing copy.

## Goals

**Business goal:** Build a high-converting Nevada personal-injury lead funnel.

**Conversion action:** Complete the calculator and submit contact information for follow-up.

**Known future needs:** Backend hardening, Supabase database/auth, lead capture, email notifications, admin dashboard, and lead management.
